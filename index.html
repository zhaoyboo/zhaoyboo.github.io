I am a PhD student in Computer Science at the University of Maryland, advised by Prof. Zaoxing (Alan) Liu in the FROOT Lab. I build efficient systems for the reliability, safety, and observability of LLM serving.

Previously, I worked on networked systems. I received my B.Eng. in Computer Science from ShanghaiTech University.

Research

Safety & quality monitoring

Online monitors that fuse model-internal signals to detect low-quality outputs such as hallucinations at serving time, with low overhead.

Serving reliability

Fuzzing LLM serving engines (vLLM, SGLang) to uncover concurrency bugs and vulnerabilities.

Observability

Performant, flexible inspection of model internals during LLM inference.
